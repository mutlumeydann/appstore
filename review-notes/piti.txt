App: Piti Piti: Finger Chooser

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached to this submission (App Review Information > Attachment). It starts from launching the app on the Home Screen and shows the typical user flow. The app has no account registration/login, no user-generated content and no paid content inside the app.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Piti Piti is a "finger chooser" for groups of friends, families and classrooms. When people need to decide fairly and quickly who goes first, who pays, who does a chore or how to split into teams, everyone puts one finger on the screen and the app chooses randomly with a spinning-light animation. It replaces arguments, coin flips and drawing straws with an instant, visibly fair result. Target audience: anyone in a group setting, all ages (rated 4+).

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
- Launch the app. The home screen is ready immediately; no setup or permissions are required.
- Choose a mode with the buttons at the bottom: 1 WINNER (first out), 1 WINNER (last left), 2 TEAMS, or ORDER.
- Two or more people each place one finger on the screen and hold still. After about 2 seconds the lights spin, slow down and stop on the chosen finger(s), with sound and vibration.
- Lift all fingers to start a new round. Sound can be turned off from the speaker icon.
- To test alone, simply place two or more of your own fingers on the screen.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app works fully offline. It makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs (no analytics, advertising, authentication, payment, cloud or AI services). There is no account, no sign-in and no in-app purchase; the app is a one-time paid download.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions. The interface is localized into 14 languages and follows the device language automatically (it can also be changed inside the app).

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. All graphics, icons and sounds were created specifically for this app.
