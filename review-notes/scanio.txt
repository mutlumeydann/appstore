App: Scanio: PDF Document Scanner

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached to this submission (App Review Information > Attachment). It starts from launching the app on the Home Screen and shows the typical user flow. The app has no account registration/login, no user-generated content and no paid content inside the app.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Scanio is a document scanner that turns paper (receipts, invoices, contracts, notes, forms) into clean multi-page PDFs that can be shared or printed. It solves the everyday need to digitize and send paper documents without a physical scanner, and it keeps everything private on the device with no account or subscription. Target audience: students, professionals and households, all ages (rated 4+).

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
- Launch the app. The Documents library opens; no account is needed.
- Tap Scan. The iOS document camera (Apple VisionKit) opens and detects page edges automatically; capture one or more pages and tap Save. Camera permission is requested on first use.
- The new document opens. Tap Share PDF to export via the share sheet, or Print. Tap Edit to rotate, reorder or delete pages; tap the title to rename.
- The Photos button imports existing images from the photo library into a document (photo access is requested on first use).
- Settings (icon at the top right of the library): paper size A4/Letter, PDF quality, optional Face ID / passcode lock, language.
- All documents are stored only on the device.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app works fully offline. It makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs (no analytics, advertising, authentication, payment, cloud or AI services). There is no account, no sign-in and no in-app purchase; the app is a one-time paid download.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions. The interface is localized into 14 languages and follows the device language automatically (it can also be changed inside the app).

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. All graphics, icons and sounds were created specifically for this app.
